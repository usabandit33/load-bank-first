FAQ

What is a load bank test?

A controlled resistive or reactive load applied to the generator to verify it can carry its nameplate rating and clear wet-stacking.

How often?

Often annual for commercial; more frequent for life-safety. Confirm with your AHJ and the manufacturer interval.

Can I just do oil changes?

Oil and filters are part of maintenance. Load bank is a performance test. They are not the same line item.
